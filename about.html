Meet Orange

Part citrus, part mortgage genius, fully committed to getting Orange County into their dream homes. He's the friendly face of West Coast Capital Mortgage, Inc. — here to make home financing simple, smart, and a little more fun.

Origin Story

How a citrus became a closer

Every great mascot has a humble beginning. Orange's just happens to involve a grove, a tiny blue suit, and an unhealthy obsession with interest rates.

Day One

Born in an Orange County grove 🌳

Orange popped off the branch with a leaf on his head, big curious eyes, and a feeling that he was destined for something bigger than a fruit bowl.

The Glow-Up

Found the navy suit 👔

One day he tried on a sharp navy suit and orange tie. It fit perfectly. The little house pin on the lapel? That was the moment he knew: mortgages.

The Calling

Fell head over peel for home loans 🏡

While neighbors were busy being juice, Orange was reading rate sheets, comparing loan programs, and helping families figure out what they could afford.

Today

Orange County's friendliest advisor ⭐

Now he guides buyers and homeowners from pre-approval to keys-in-hand — backed by the licensed team at West Coast Capital Mortgage, Inc.
