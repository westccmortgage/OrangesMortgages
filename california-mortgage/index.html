Questions

Frequently asked

How much do I need for a down payment in California?

It depends on the program. Conventional loans can start as low as 3% down, FHA at 3.5%, and VA loans at $0 down for eligible borrowers. We'll walk you through options based on your goals and budget.

What is a jumbo loan and when do I need one?

A jumbo loan is financing above the conforming limit for your county. In many high-cost California markets, homes commonly require jumbo or high-balance financing — an area we specialize in.

I'm self-employed. Can I still qualify?

Yes. Bank statement and DSCR programs let qualified borrowers use bank deposits or rental cash flow instead of traditional tax-return income.

How long does pre-approval take?

For many borrowers, pre-approval can be issued within 24 hours once we have your basic information and documentation.
