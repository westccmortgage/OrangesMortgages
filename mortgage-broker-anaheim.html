Questions

Anaheim mortgage FAQ

Is Anaheim good for first-time buyers?

Yes — Anaheim's more attainable price points pair well with low-down-payment programs (conventional 3%, FHA 3.5%, VA 0%) and California down-payment assistance.

What loan programs work best in Anaheim?

Many Anaheim buyers use conventional, FHA, or VA loans, since prices often fit within conforming limits. We'll match you to the best fit for your budget and credit.

How much down payment do I need in Anaheim?

Often less than you think — as low as 3% conventional, 3.5% FHA, or 0% VA for eligible buyers, plus possible assistance. See our down payment guide for details.
