# Review request kit — Orange Mortgage / West Coast Capital Mortgage

Reviews are the #1 lever for local ranking **and** conversion. Goal: go from 4 → 20+
Google reviews. The assets:

- **Review page (live):** `https://orangesmortgages.com/review` — big "Leave a Google Review" button + a scannable QR.
- **QR code:** `assets/review-qr.png` (and `.svg`) — drop it on business cards, email signatures, closing packets, flyers. It points to the review page.

## Step 0 — get your 1-tap Google review link (2 min, do this once)
1. Go to your **Google Business Profile** (business.google.com) → **"Ask for reviews"** (or "Get more reviews").
2. Copy the short link — it looks like `https://g.page/r/XXXXXXXX/review`.
3. Send it to me and I'll wire it into the review page button (right now the button uses a
   working fallback that opens your Google listing; the `g.page/r/...` link makes it truly 1-tap).

## When to ask
Right after a **positive moment**: at closing, when the loan funds, or when a client thanks
you. That's when people are happiest and most willing.

## Best practices
- Ask **personally** (name + specific detail). Generic blasts convert poorly.
- Make it **one tap** — always include the direct link or QR.
- **Never** offer payment/incentives for reviews, and never write fake ones — Google can suspend the profile.
- **Reply to every review** within a day or two (positive and negative), professionally.

## Handling a negative review
1. Don't panic or argue publicly. Reply calmly, thank them, and offer to make it right offline.
2. Example: *"Thank you for the feedback, [Name]. I'm sorry your experience fell short of what
   we aim for. I'd like to understand and make it right — please call me directly at (310) 654-1577."*
3. A professional response to criticism often impresses future readers more than a perfect score.
