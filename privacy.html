Privacy Policy

What Orange Mortgage collects when you use this website or send us a request, and how it is used.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Who we are

Orange Mortgage (orangesmortgages.com) is a website of West Coast Capital Mortgage, NMLS #2817729, CA DRE Corporation License #02440065, 150 E Olive Ave, Unit 112, Burbank, CA 91502.

What we collect

When you send the "Ask Orange" form we receive the details you type in: your first and last name, email address, phone number (optional), what you are looking to do, and your message. The form also records where you came from: the page you landed on, the page you sent the form from, and advertising or campaign parameters in the link you used (for example UTM values or an ad click ID).

Your browser also stores your language choice and these campaign parameters on your device so the form can include them. You can clear them in your browser settings.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and follow up with you by phone call and email about it.
  • To understand which pages and ads lead to requests, so we can improve the site.

We do not ask for a Social Security number, bank details or passwords on this website. Please do not send them in the message field.

Advertising and measurement

This site uses Google Ads tags to measure visits and requests. These tags may also be used to show you ads elsewhere on the web. You can manage or turn off ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

Hosting

The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which handles technical request data (such as IP address and browser type) in order to serve pages and protect the site.

Company policy

Requests are handled by West Coast Capital Mortgage. For how the company handles personal information more broadly, see its Privacy Policy.

California residents

If you live in California, you may ask us for access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Call (310) 654-1577 or email orange@orangesmortgages.com.
