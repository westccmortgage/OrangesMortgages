Irvine mortgage FAQ

Can you help me buy a home in Irvine?

Yes — West Coast Capital Mortgage (Orange Mortgage) helps buyers, homeowners, and investors throughout Irvine and all of Orange County, with conventional, FHA, VA, jumbo, and investment loans.

Do I need a jumbo loan to buy in Irvine?

Not always. Irvine spans many price points, and Orange County's conforming limit is high — plenty of buyers qualify for conforming or high-balance financing. It's worth checking your county line before assuming jumbo.

How fast can I get pre-approved in Irvine?

Often within 24–48 hours once we have your basic documents — important in Irvine's competitive, multiple-offer market.
