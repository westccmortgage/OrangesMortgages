Why Orange County financing is different

Orange County isn't an average market, and an average mortgage approach won't cut it. A few things local buyers should know:

  • Prices push many loans into jumbo territory — but not always. The conforming limit is high here, so it pays to check your county line before assuming jumbo. See our jumbo vs conforming guide.
  • Offers are competitive. A fast, solid pre-approval makes your offer credible to sellers.
  • You may need less down than you think. Even in a high-priced market, low-down-payment programs and assistance exist.

Questions

Orange County mortgage FAQ

Do you offer mortgages throughout Orange County?

Yes — we help buyers, homeowners, and investors across Orange County (Irvine, Newport Beach, Huntington Beach, Anaheim, Costa Mesa, Fullerton, Laguna Beach, Mission Viejo) and throughout California.

Why do so many Orange County buyers need a jumbo loan?

OC home prices are among the highest in the country, so many loan amounts exceed the county conforming limit. But the limit is high here, so plenty of buyers still qualify for conforming or high-balance financing — check your county line first.

How fast can I get pre-approved?

Often within 24–48 hours once we have your basic documents. In a competitive OC market, a ready pre-approval helps your offer stand out.

What types of home loans do you offer?

Conventional, FHA, VA, jumbo, DSCR (investment), and refinance loans, plus options for self-employed borrowers.
